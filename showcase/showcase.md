# Carom

A paddle, a ball, and a court full of angles. Carom is a fast paddle duel where a clean return is only the beginning. Face the computer or challenge a friend on the same keyboard.

Move into the hit and give the ball spin. A downward sweep bends its flight clockwise; an upward sweep bends it the other way. Each return picks up speed, making a long rally a test of touch and timing.

Two amber obstacles split the court into bank-shot opportunities. Send the ball around them, bounce it off an edge, or let a curved shot find a surprising route toward the goal. Reading the next bounce matters as much as reaching the ball.

Every point brings a fresh serve toward the player who conceded it. Race to eleven, then earn a two-point lead to finish the match. At ten-all, hold your nerve: the next rally could turn everything around.
